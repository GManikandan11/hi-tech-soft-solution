- GST billing and tax management.

## How to Install and Set Up TallyPrime

Getting started with TallyPrime begins with downloading, installing, and configuring the software according to your business requirements.

A proper installation and initial setup help businesses use the software efficiently from the beginning.

### Step 1: Download TallyPrime

Download the appropriate TallyPrime installer from the official Tally Solutions website. Make sure your computer meets the software's system requirements before installation.

### Step 2: Install the Software

Run the downloaded installer and follow the installation instructions. Select the appropriate installation location and complete the setup process.

### Step 3: Activate Your Licence

Choose the applicable licensing option and complete the activation process using your valid Tally licence credentials.

### Step 4: Create Your Company

Enter your business name, address, country, state, financial year, and other required company details.

### Step 5: Configure Accounting and GST

Create accounting ledgers, configure GST details, and set up the necessary financial information based on your business activities.

### Step 6: Start Managing Business Transactions

Once the initial configuration is complete, start recording sales, purchases, payments, receipts, and other daily business transactions.

## Important TallyPrime Features for Business Management

TallyPrime provides several useful features that help businesses manage their accounting and daily operations.

Maintaining accurate financial records is important for understanding business performance. TallyPrime helps businesses record transactions and monitor their financial activities.

- Profit and loss statements.

### 2. GST Billing and Tax Management

GST billing is an important requirement for many businesses in India. TallyPrime provides GST-related features that help businesses generate invoices and maintain tax transaction records.

Important GST features include:

- GST company configuration.
- GST sales and purchase invoices.
- Tax ledger management.
- GST reports.
- E-invoicing support.
- E-Way Bill functionality.

Correct GST configuration helps businesses organise their tax records and prepare relevant reports.

### 3. Inventory and Stock Management

Inventory management is essential for retail shops, wholesalers, distributors, and trading companies. TallyPrime helps businesses track stock movements and maintain organised inventory records.

Important inventory features include:

- Stock item and stock group creation.
- Godown and warehouse management.
- Purchase and sales stock tracking.
- Stock quantity monitoring.
- Unit of measurement configuration.
- Inventory valuation and reporting.

These features help businesses monitor available stock and make better purchasing decisions.

### 4. Sales and Purchase Management

Managing daily sales and purchase transactions becomes easier when all records are maintained in one system.

TallyPrime helps businesses record invoices, track customer transactions, and manage supplier payments.

Businesses can use it for:

- Sales invoice generation.
- Purchase invoice recording.
- Customer outstanding management.
- Supplier payment tracking.
- Credit and debit note entries.
- Sales and purchase reports.

An organised transaction system helps improve accounting accuracy and business record management.
