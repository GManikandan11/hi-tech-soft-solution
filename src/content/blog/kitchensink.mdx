## <a name="Paragraphs"></a>Managing Daily Business Transactions

Every business handles multiple transactions throughout the day. Recording them correctly helps maintain accurate accounts and reduces confusion during financial reviews.

In TallyPrime, businesses can record sales, purchases, receipts, payments, journal entries and other accounting transactions.

For example, when a customer purchases products on credit, the sales invoice can be recorded against the customer's ledger. When the customer makes a payment later, the receipt transaction can be entered to update the outstanding balance.

Similarly, supplier purchases and payments can be recorded to maintain accurate supplier account information.

Regular accounting entries make it easier to review business transactions and identify pending payments.

Another important activity is maintaining proper expense records. Expenses such as rent, electricity, salaries, transportation and maintenance should be recorded under suitable ledgers.

This helps business owners understand where their money is being spent and review the overall profitability of their business.

## <a name="Code"></a>Accounting Entries and Examples

### Understanding a Sales Transaction

When a business sells products to a customer, the transaction should be recorded in the sales voucher.

For example, consider a business selling products worth ₹10,000 before applicable GST.

The accounting entry will depend on the GST rate and the nature of the transaction.

### Example of a Basic Accounting Entry

```text
Customer Account       Dr.   ₹11,800
    To Sales Account          ₹10,000
    To Output GST Account      ₹1,800
```

<text>This is an illustrative example assuming an 18% GST rate for a taxable transaction. Actual entries depend on the applicable tax treatment and transaction details.</text>

### Example of a Payment Entry

```text
Rent Account           Dr.   ₹10,000
    To Cash Account           ₹10,000
```

These examples demonstrate the basic accounting structure. Actual voucher selection and ledger configuration should match the business transaction.

## <a name="Inline"></a>Useful Accounting Practices

Maintaining accurate accounting records requires consistency. Businesses should make it a habit to review transactions and update their financial information regularly.

Here are some practical recommendations:

- **Record transactions daily** instead of waiting until the end of the month.
- **Create suitable ledgers** for customers, suppliers, income and expenses.
- **Check GST details** before recording taxable transactions.
- **Update inventory records** whenever stock is purchased or sold.
- **Review outstanding balances** to follow up on pending payments.
- **Take regular backups** to protect important company data.
